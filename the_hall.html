The Great Hall

A Place of Legend

Our History

Carw Gwyn, or "White Stag," stands on grounds that have been a gathering place for centuries. Legend says that a white stag once led a lost prince to this very spot, where he found a spring of pure water and a hive of wild bees. Today, we honor that legend by crafting the finest mead and welcoming travelers from near and far.

The Hearth

At the center of our hall lies the Great Hearth, a roaring fire that never goes out. It is the heart of Carw Gwyn, where stories are told, friendships are forged, and the cold of the outside world is forgotten.

Private Hire

Wish to host your own clan gathering? The Hall is available for private events, weddings, and feasts. Let us provide the mead, the music, and the atmosphere for your special occasion.
