The Great Hall

A Place of Legend

A Sanctuary from the Modern World

♦

In Welsh mythology, the Carw Gwyn (pronounced “ca-roo gwinn”, White Stag) is a messenger from the Otherworld—a sign that one is ready to embark on a spiritual quest. Here in the heart of Denver, your journey leads you to us.

Carw Gwyn is more than a bar; it is a return to the ancient traditions of the gathering hall. Constructed from reclaimed timber and styled after the great halls of the 12th century, we invite you to leave the digital age at the door. Whether you seek the solace of a quiet corner for real conversation, or the raucous joy of a long table feast, you will find your place by our hearth.

Come for the warmth of the fire, the scent of roasted meats, and the golden glint of honey wine. Welcome to the Hall of the White Stag.

The Hearth

At the center of our hall lies the Great Hearth, a roaring fire that never goes out. It is the heart of Carw Gwyn, where stories are told, friendships are forged, and the cold of the outside world is forgotten.

Private Hire

Wish to host your own clan gathering? The Hall is available for private events, weddings, and feasts. Let us provide the mead, the music, and the atmosphere for your special occasion.
